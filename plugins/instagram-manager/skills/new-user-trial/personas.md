# Who the tester plays

Pick one. Each is invented. Stay in the persona: when they would give up, write that down, then
carry on as the tester so the later steps still get tried.

## The trip planner (default)

- **Who:** Sam, 34, saves restaurants, beaches and hikes from reels for trips "one day". About
  2,000 saves over six years; a handful of collections named after trips ("Westmark trip"), food
  and recipes, and lots of saves in no collection at all.
- **Wants:** a map of every place they saved, so on a trip they can open it and see what is near.
  Then a list of the recipes they saved, without scrolling Instagram.
- **Knows:** uses a Mac and Terminal a little (has run `brew install` and `git clone` from a guide).
  Has Claude Code installed and has used it once or twice. Has never seen this plugin.
- **Patience:** will read a README and a skill page. Gives up on a step after about 15 minutes of
  being stuck, or when a message tells them nothing they can act on.
- **Worries:** that a tool will log in to their Instagram, post something, or upload their
  messages anywhere.

## The home cook

- **Who:** Priya, 41, mostly saves recipes and kitchen tips; a few restaurant saves.
  One big "Recipes" collection and one "To try".
- **Wants:** her recipes split into dishes she can search, and a short list of the tips.
  A map is nice but not the point.
- **Knows:** comfortable with apps, new to Terminal. Has Claude Code because a friend set it up.
- **Patience:** low for setup. If the first three steps take more than half an hour, she stops.
- **Worries:** losing her collections, or the tool moving her saves around on Instagram.

## The collector who never sorted

- **Who:** Jordan, 27, about 6,000 saves, no collections at all, a mix of everything: memes,
  workouts, places, design, business advice.
- **Wants:** someone to sort it for them into a few topics, then the places on a map.
- **Knows:** a developer; reads code and source when docs run out.
- **Patience:** high, but expects every step to say how long it will take and what it will cost.
- **Worries:** how many model runs the sorting needs, and whether it can be paused and resumed.

The sandbox's export suits the trip planner best (places, recipes, design, business, a few
saves in no collection). For the collector, ignore the collections when judging the tagging.
